Privacy Policy

Stack & Snap · Effective 4 September 2026

Summary

Stack & Snap is a free game suitable for all ages. You can play it entirely without an account. It shows no ads and sells no data.

What we collect

  • Playing without signing in: nothing is collected or sent anywhere. Your best score is stored only on your own device (browser local storage).
  • If you choose to sign in with Google: we receive your Google account's display name, profile photo URL, email address, and a unique user ID via Google Firebase Authentication. We store your display name, photo URL, and best score in our leaderboard database (Google Cloud Firestore) so they can appear on the public top-10 leaderboard.

What is public

Only your display name, profile photo, and best score appear on the public leaderboard. Your email address is never shown to other players and is not stored in the leaderboard database.

Third-party services

Authentication and the leaderboard are provided by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We use no advertising networks and no analytics trackers.

Children

The game is designed for all ages. Signing in is optional and never required to play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; a Google account (with its own age requirements) is needed to use the optional sign-in.

Data deletion

To remove your leaderboard entry and account data, email gharshvardhan1010@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, and it will be deleted within 30 days. You can also simply sign out at any time; nothing new is collected after that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
