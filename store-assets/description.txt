Milo is balancing a wooden plank — and everything keeps landing on it!

Stack & Snap is a cozy physics game for all ages. Drop the Snaplings — cheerful little characters with minds of their own — onto Milo's plank and keep everything balanced while his worried Mom watches from the kitchen doorway.

HOW IT PLAYS
• One-finger controls: move to aim, tap to drop. That's it!
• Real physics: the plank leans toward the heavy side — plan every drop
• Feel the tension: lean too long and the stack starts to slip… rescue it by loading the other side!
• Chain quick landings for combo bonuses
• Meet special Snaplings: heavy Chonk, bouncy Boing, tiny Pip, and the rare golden Star
• Wind gusts get fiercer as you level up — how high can you score?

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Cute hand-crafted art and characters full of personality — they blink, worry, and panic!
• Play in seconds, master over weeks
• 3 lives, milestones, celebrations, and a satisfying "one more try" loop
• Optional Google sign-in with a global top-10 leaderboard
• No ads. Free. Works offline after first play.
